# Onboarding Redesign

## Outcome

Help new administrators reach their first successful setup without a scheduled implementation call.

## Work completed

- Replaced the long setup form with a guided checklist.
- Added progress persistence and contextual help.
- Instrumented completion and abandonment events.

## Delivery notes

The redesign took ten engineering days. Analytics work was underestimated because old events were inconsistent.

## Risks and lessons

- Audit existing tracking before promising experiment dates.
- Define the activation event with Product and Analytics.
- Preserve progress when users leave and return.
