# Support Platform Migration

## Outcome

Move support conversations from the legacy help desk into a new platform without losing customer history.

## Work completed

- Exported and normalized tickets, users, and attachments.
- Recreated routing rules and service-level policies.
- Ran a staged cutover for the support team.

## Delivery notes

The migration took fifteen engineering days. Attachment formats and rate limits were the largest unknowns.

## Risks and lessons

- Sample historical attachments before committing to a timeline.
- Measure vendor API rate limits with a realistic export.
- Keep the old system read-only for a short validation period.
