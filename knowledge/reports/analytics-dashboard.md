# Analytics Dashboard

## Outcome

Give account managers a single dashboard for activation, retention, and expansion signals.

## Work completed

- Defined shared metric names with Sales and Customer Success.
- Added nightly aggregation queries.
- Built filters for plan, segment, and account owner.

## Delivery notes

The project took twelve engineering days. Metric-definition disagreements delayed acceptance by three days.

## Risks and lessons

- Agree on metric definitions before building visualizations.
- Test slow queries against production-sized sample data.
- Name a single business owner for acceptance decisions.
